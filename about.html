About Us
At Iron & Ivory Barber Co., we believe a haircut is more than a service — it’s a tradition. Our barbers combine classic techniques with modern precision to deliver sharp cuts, clean fades, and perfectly sculpted beards. Every detail matters, from the hot towel to the final styling touch. Inspired by the timeless barbershops of the past, our space is designed for comfort, confidence, and conversation. Whether you’re preparing for an important meeting or just maintaining your signature look, we make sure you leave feeling your absolute best. Iron & Ivory isn’t just a barbershop. It’s a standard.
We are open Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm. Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking an appointment to secure your spot. We look forward to welcoming you to the Iron & Ivory family.
